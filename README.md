## Hi there 👋

I'm Hannah, a researcher inspired by the natural world. I have researched **seasonally and daily recurring biological events as a basis for understanding environmental change**, with a broad goal of informing biodiversity protections and management toward a more sustainable future. 

My early work in this area addressed hormone and metabolic mechanisms that underlie how and when organisms respond to environmental stressors in the wild. My more recent work funded by an NSF Graduate Research Fellowship investigates phenology 🌸🪺🍂 and diel activity 💤🦇 through **field data analyses**, **wet lab experiments**, and **custom-built computer simulations**. Many thanks to my mentors and peers who have and continue to support me. (You know who you are.)

Currently, I am based at the University of Notre Dame, where I am exploring how thermal shifts in seasonality and day-night cycles create joint challenges and actionable opportunities for biodiversity management and environmental justice under climate change. Part of this research will draw from my time dedicated to a data-driven synthesis working group funded by the [Just Transformations to Sustainability initiative](https://sustainabilityinitiative.nd.edu/research/programs/integrated-responses-to-climate-and-biodiversity-change/) at Notre Dame in partnership with the University of Michigan's Institute for Global Change Biology.
